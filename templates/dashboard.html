Multi-Stage Neural Architecture Pipeline:
1
Stage 1: Foliar & Morphological Gate

Rejects non-leaf photos (skin, wood, plastics, blanks) via green chloroplast pigmentation index ($G > 1.04R \land G > 1.04B \ge 0.03$) and edge texture standard deviation threshold (σ ≥ 14.0).

2
Stage 2: 128-D Cosine Embedding Centroid Gate

Extracts normalized feature vectors from MobileNetV2 layer crop_features. Computes cosine similarity to calibrated multi-prototype centroids (`crop_centroids.json`) to isolate unsupported crop species.

3
Stage 3: Crop-Constrained Sliced Softmax Re-normalization

Slices logits strictly to the validated crop species, eliminating cross-crop misclassification and ensuring combined probability $P(\text{crop}) \times P(\text{disease} \mid \text{crop})$.
